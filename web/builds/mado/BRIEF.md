4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See below.

6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** User: playful. → light cream ground, stickers with hard offset shadows, tilted cards.

**Authored silence:** none.

## Grammar

Filmic one-shot: one short arc for a cafe brand. The other grammars lost: chaptered editorial is too serious, live surface has no product UI, continuous world is too fragile, the typographic poster has no hero object, a gallery covers only the menu, split stage has no two-sided argument, and a rhythmic cutlist can't give the explode room to land.
