The anatomy

What's in the cup?

  • Ice Lots of it. Iced is our default.
  • Ceremonial matcha Stone-ground in Japan, whisked by hand for every cup.
  • Milk, your way Full cream, or swap to oat, almond, soy, coconut, skim or lactose-free.
  • The cup Every one wears a now-playing label.

Leaf to powder, the slow way.

01 · Shade

About three weeks under black cloth before harvest. Less sun, sweeter leaves.

02 · Pick

Only the first spring flush, only the youngest leaves.

03 · Steam

Steamed within hours, so the green stays green.

04 · Dry

Stems and veins out. What's left is called tencha.

05 · Grind

Granite stones, about forty grams an hour. Then it's ours to whisk.
